C. Independent Pixel Queries: Additional Diag-
    nostics

The following diagnostics supplement the qualitative analy-
sis in Sec. 6.1.2. Across five subjects, the assembled pictures
have high spatial autocorrelation (Moran’s I of luminance
0.85–0.96, against 0.01 when the same colors are shuffled)
and share coarse composition with whole-image Canvas 2D
drawings of the same subjects (luminance correlation 0.51–
0.88). Re-asking the same coordinate returns the identical
color only 2–38% of the time, though the difference is small
(about 10 of 255 per channel), and fine structure such as
facial features or the leaves of a branch does not survive.

D. Self-Furnished Room Protocol

D.1. Self-Furnished Rooms

The experiment in Sec. 6.2 uses 30 rooms from the
ProcTHOR-10K validation split. The agent receives a room
type, rough shape, and a list of 7–14 furniture pieces de-
scribed with size words, without furniture positions.   It
chooses an arrangement, draws an initial wall view as SVG
with one group per piece, and follows six quarter- and half-
turns. The turn sequence visits every wall, revisits headings,
and ends facing north. A final top-down plan provides a
second expression of the agent’s chosen arrangement.
  The four conditions retain earlier SVGs, replace them
with one-line stubs, replace both the SVGs and the furniture
list with stubs, or use a fresh call for each view with only the
furniture list and turn history. Every condition covers all 30
rooms.
