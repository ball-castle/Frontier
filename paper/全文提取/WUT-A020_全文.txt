Figure 4. Overview of the proposed MDIC framework. The input view Ix is lossy-compressed, while the correlated view Iy is transmitted
losslessly as side information available only at the decoder. Both Ix and Iy are encoded into latent spaces by the LDM encoder [27],
producing zLx and zLy, which are further refined by the HyperAlign module into zx and zy. VQ-VAE quantization [30] is applied for
compression and feature clustering, yielding ˆzx and ˆzy. A Transformer-based autoregressive entropy model [32] estimates the bitrate
bppx. The BLIP-2 captioning model [13] generates textual descriptions from Iy, which are then encoded into ztext to provide global
semantic guidance. The Arithmetic Encoding (AE) further converts the ˆzx, ˆzy, zy, and ztext into a binary bitstream, with the Arithmetic
Decoding (AD) performing the inverse operation. Under the supervision of ztext, a visual mask mv is generated and integrated into the
Mask-Gated Side Information Supplementary Module (MG-SISM): gate G1 fuses ˆzy to restore categorical information lost in ˆzx, while
gate G2 extracts fine-grained object details from zy. After a denoising process guided by multimodal side information (zd, ˆzxc, and ztext),
the final reconstruction Iˆx is obtained by decoding the denoised latent variable ˆz0 with the LDM decoder.
