Abstract  Image matting is of great importance in both
computer vision and graphics applications.  Most exist-
ing state-of-the-art techniques rely on large sparse matri-
ces such as the matting Laplacian [12]. However, solving
these linear systems is often time-consuming, which is un-
favored for the user interaction. In this paper, we propose a
                                                                                         (a)                                   (b)
fast method for high quality matting. We ﬁrst derive an efﬁ-
cient algorithm to solve a large kernel matting Laplacian. A
large kernel propagates information more quickly and may
improve the matte quality. To further reduce running time,
we also use adaptive kernel sizes by a KD-tree trimap seg-
mentation technique. A variety of experiments show that
our algorithm provides high quality results and is 5 to 20
                                                                                         (c)                                   (d)
times faster than previous methods.
