Figure 2. Conceptual comparison of four unsupervised learning mechanisms applied to video. The inputs consist of ρ=2 clips from
B videos. Each clip is a stack of T frames with temporal stride τ and spatial resolution S2. Each method trains encoder weights θ by
computing a positive loss component w.r.t. to the other clips of the same video. SimCLR (a) and MoCo (b) use a contrastive loss with
negatives coming from different videos in the batch or a a queue. respectively. MoCo (b) and BYOL (c) use extra momentum encoders with
weights θm being moving averages of the trained θ. SwAV (d) uses a Sinkhorn-Knop (SK) transform to generate the positive targets.
